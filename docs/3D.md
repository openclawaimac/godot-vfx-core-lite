# 2D effects in 3D scenes

> **Lite edition.** Effect ids marked *(Pro only)* ship in the full pack (VFX Core 2D for Godot 4).
> They are not in Lite: `VfxCore.has(id)` returns false for them, and `play()` reports an unknown effect id.

VFX Core 2D is a 2D effect library. It has two honest ways to put its effects in a 3D game.
Neither makes an effect volumetric: an effect is always a flat 2D image.

| | `VCOverlay3D` | `VCBillboard3D` (1.1) |
|---|---|---|
| How | Draws on top of the 3D view at the projected screen point. | A camera-facing card (Sprite3D) in the world, textured by a small transparent SubViewport. |
| Depth | **No depth test**: effects show through walls. | **Depth-tested**: geometry in front hides the effect. |
| Size | Scales by camera distance (`reference_distance`, clamped). | Real world size (`world_size` in metres); perspective does the rest. |
| Cost | Same as 2D. | One SubViewport per card (384×384 by default) plus the effect. Reuse a few cards instead of making one per shot. |
| Lines, beams | Supported (end points re-projected; small glue). | Only inside the card's square. |
| Best for | RTS / top-down cameras, UI-like callouts, many small effects. | Third-person or free cameras where occlusion matters. |

Demo: `demo/billboard_3d.tscn` (F6). Space pauses all effects (`VfxCore.paused`), F toggles 4× fast-forward.

## `VCBillboard3D` in short

```gdscript
var bb := VCBillboard3D.new()
bb.world_size = 4.0              # the square covers 4 m
bb.origin = Vector2(0.5, 0.85)   # effect origin near the bottom, for fire and smoke
add_child(bb)
bb.position = Vector3(0, 1, 0)
# bb.play(&"fire_large")           # same opts as VfxCore.play()  (Pro only)
```

Effects are played through `VfxCore.play_attached()` on an anchor in the card's own
CanvasLayer, so pooling, the particle budget, `time_scale`, `paused` and `suspended` all apply.
Freeing the card ends its effects and returns them to the pool.

## Which effects suit 3D

Checked by eye in the 3D evaluation captures (`tools/capture/overlay3d.gd`, `demo/billboard_3d.tscn`).

| Family | Overlay | Billboard | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| Explosions, fire, smoke, steam | good | good | Read as volumes from any angle. Set `origin` low for rising effects. |
| Hits, sparks, muzzles, pickups, bursts (heal, frost, death) | good | good | Short and round; good billboard material. |
| Teleports, status loops | good | good | Status: put the card on the unit (a child Node3D) so it moves with it. |
| Debris | fair | fair | Falls to the 2D `floor_y` of the card, not onto 3D ground. |
| Beams, lightning, trails | fair (glue) | poor | Long and directional; the card clips them and turns them with the camera. |
| Shock rings, magic circles, ground decals, scorch marks | poor | poor | Meant to lie on the ground; both paths show them facing the camera. Use a decal or a flat quad with your own texture. |
| Weather, screen effects (flash, vignette), UI and text | n/a | n/a | Play them in 2D on top of the 3D view (plain `VfxCore.play` / `play_attached` on a Control). |

## Limits (by design)

- An effect is a flat card. It never lies on the ground, has no thickness and turns with the camera.
- It does not light the 3D scene (2D lights stay in the card) and receives no 3D lighting.
- The card clips the effect at its square: size `world_size` and `origin` to the effect.
- Additive glows are composited from a transparent viewport; they look right over dark and mid
  backgrounds but can look slightly weaker over bright skies than in 2D.
- Pixel-art style works, but the card is filtered (linear) by default; set `texture_filter`
  to nearest for crisp pixels.
