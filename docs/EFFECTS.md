## How to read this table

- **usage** is the call the effect is built for:
  - `play`: one-shot or loop at a world position.
  - `line`: between two points or along a path (`play_line`, or the `points` option).
  - `attached`: follows a Node2D (`play_attached`).
  - `ui`: on a Control. The effect is hosted in the Control's CanvasLayer and never changes its layout.
  - `text`: shows the `text` option (at most 32 characters).
  - `screen`: full-screen or camera effect; the position is ignored.
  - `apply`: a material effect on a CanvasItem (`VfxCore.apply`); the original material comes back when it ends.
- **size** is the particle class from the spec: S has at most 32 particles, M at most 64, L at most 128, all at intensity 1.
- **showcase** is the scale the demo's "Showcase size" checkbox and the store captures use, so that small effects read at store size.
  - Gameplay defaults are always scale 1. Pass `{"scale": n}` to match your sprites.
  - The demo's code snippet always shows the gameplay call.
- **presets**: `light_bg` presets are tuned for pale backgrounds; additive glows vanish on white.
- **Loops** run until `handle.stop()`, and their duration is one cycle. Everything else is a one-shot that emits `finished`.

## Footprints (scale 1)

The radius that holds 90% of the clearly visible pixels, measured by `tools/capture/footprint.sh`:

| effect | blast (first 0.6 s) | overall, including smoke |
|---|---|---|
| `explosion_small` | 100 px | 114 px |

`VCHookDestruction` picks the size by detonation radius (`radius_medium` 72, `radius_large` 140) and scales the effect by radius / threshold. The visible blast is therefore about 1.4 to 1.7 times the gameplay radius, which matches the debris throw. To keep the fireball inside the damage radius, raise `radius_medium` and `radius_large`: the scale is radius / threshold, so higher thresholds play smaller blasts.
