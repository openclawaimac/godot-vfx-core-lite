# Changelog (Lite)

## 1.1.0 (2026-11-02)

- Time control: `VfxCore.paused` freezes all effects, `VfxCore.suspended` drops new plays during fast-forward or catch-up loops, and `VfxCore.time_scale` now reaches the particle simulation.
- 3D: `VCBillboard3D` shows effects on depth-tested camera-facing cards in a 3D scene; demo `demo/billboard_3d.tscn`, guide `docs/3D.md`.

## 1.0.1 (2026-11-02)

- README: the link to the full pack points at its store page, https://huginhelm.itch.io/vfx-core-2d-godot-4.

## 1.0.0 (2026-10-28)

- First Lite build: 8 effects + `hit_flash`, full runtime, Destruction Core hook, demo browser. MIT.
